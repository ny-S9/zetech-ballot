How it works

  1. Apply. You apply for your own department, and only students from your department can vote for you.
  2. Review. The Dean's office approves your application.
  3. Students vote. Students vote for delegates of their own department.
  4. Electoral college. The top candidates of each department become delegates and vote for the executives.
